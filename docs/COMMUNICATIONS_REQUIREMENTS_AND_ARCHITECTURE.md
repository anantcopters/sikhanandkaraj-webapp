# SikhanandKaraj Communications Requirements and Architecture

## 1. Document purpose

This document defines the product requirements, business rules, user experience, channel strategy, implemented communication baseline and target architecture for communications generated by SikhanandKaraj.

It is the source requirement and QA reference for:

- In-app/Application Notifications
- Email
- SMS
- WhatsApp messaging
- Member communication preferences
- Administrative communication tooling
- Communication audit and delivery monitoring
- Template preview and QA

Implementation-specific details are recorded where they have become approved project architecture and must be preserved by later phases.

# 2. Product context

SikhanandKaraj is a matrimonial platform. Communication behaviour must therefore be designed differently from a generic social network or marketing platform.

A matrimonial communication may disclose that:

- a person has a matrimonial profile;
- somebody expressed matrimonial Interest;
- an Interest was accepted or declined;
- somebody interacted with a profile;
- a member submitted Aadhaar or Video Introduction verification;
- a profile or media item was moderated;
- a membership was purchased or is expiring;
- a member created or received a response to a support request.

These facts can be privacy-sensitive, particularly when notifications appear on a shared mobile phone, family email account, lock screen, SMS inbox or WhatsApp account.

The platform must therefore optimize for:

1. Timely communication of important matrimonial events.
2. Privacy and member safety.
3. Trustworthy transactional communication.
4. Low communication fatigue.
5. Appropriate Free-to-Paid conversion without harassment.
6. Reliable delivery and operational visibility.
7. Consistent business-event identity across channels.
8. Long-term maintainability as communication volume grows.

# 3. Current-state baseline

## 3.1 Implemented in-app/Application Notification foundation

SikhanandKaraj has a persistent authenticated member-notification channel displayed through the existing notification bell and notification-list UI.

The approved architecture deliberately keeps this persistence/read-state channel separate from communication-event orchestration:

- `CommunicationEventRegistry` owns stable business-event identifiers.
- `MemberNotificationService` is the application boundary for creating and reading member-visible in-app notifications.
- `MemberNotificationModel` owns only persistence/query behaviour and must not define a second set of business-event constants.
- `member_notifications` stores the member-visible notification record, target, read state and history.
- Existing notification bell/list UI is reused for all supported in-app events.

This separation is intentional:

```text
Business Event Identity
CommunicationEventRegistry
          |
          +-----------------------------+
          |                             |
          v                             v
In-app/Application channel        External channels
MemberNotificationService         Email / SMS / future WhatsApp
          |
          v
member_notifications
          |
          v
Bell / Notification Centre
```

`communication_events` and `member_notifications` have different responsibilities:

- `communication_events` represents durable business-event/orchestration work where that architecture is used.
- `member_notifications` represents persistent member-facing in-app notification state including read/unread history.

They must not be merged merely because both relate to communication.

### Implemented in-app business-event types

The in-app notification service supports the central registry identifiers required by current application flows, including:

- `MESSAGE`
- `INTEREST_RECEIVED`
- `INTEREST_ACCEPTED`
- `INTEREST_DECLINED`
- `PROFILE_VIEWED`
- `PROFILE_SHORTLISTED`
- `PHOTO_APPROVED`
- `PHOTO_REJECTED`
- `AADHAAR_APPROVED`
- `AADHAAR_REJECTED`
- `VIDEO_APPROVED`
- `VIDEO_REJECTED`
- `VIDEO_RESUBMISSION_REQUESTED`
- `SUPPORT_RECEIVED`
- `SUPPORT_RESOLVED`
- `MEMBERSHIP_ACTIVATED`
- `MEMBERSHIP_EXPIRING_SOON`
- `MEMBERSHIP_EXPIRED`
- `SYSTEM`

Support in the notification service does not by itself mean every event is currently produced as an immediate bell notification. Producer behaviour remains a product decision. In particular, high-volume `PROFILE_VIEWED` and `PROFILE_SHORTLISTED` events currently participate in communication-event/digest architecture rather than being treated as mandatory immediate bell notifications.

### Implemented moderation notifications

Current moderation flows generate in-app notifications for:

- Profile photo approved.
- Profile photo rejected.
- Bulk pending profile photos approved: one member notification for the bulk action, not one notification per photo.
- Aadhaar approved.
- Aadhaar rejected.
- Video Introduction approved.
- Video Introduction rejected.
- Video Introduction resubmission requested.

Aadhaar does not currently have a separate `REQUEST_RESUBMISSION` moderation state. Rejection is the current workflow through which the member can review the reason and upload again. A separate Aadhaar resubmission notification/event must not be invented unless the Aadhaar state machine and product flow are explicitly extended.

### Implemented Interest notifications

Current Interest workflows generate in-app notifications for:

- Interest received.
- Interest accepted.
- Interest declined.

The canonical declined event is `INTEREST_DECLINED`; a separate notification-only `INTEREST_REJECTED` taxonomy must not be reintroduced.

## 3.2 In-app persistence and security rules

`MemberNotificationService` must remain the application boundary for member notification creation/read operations.

Required rules:

- Recipient user ID must be a valid positive member identifier.
- Notification type must be explicitly allowlisted from `CommunicationEventRegistry`.
- Title and message length must be bounded server-side.
- Optional actor/entity identifiers must be normalized server-side.
- Notification target URLs must be internal application routes only.
- Absolute URLs, protocol-relative URLs and scheme-based URLs must not be accepted as notification targets.
- Reading a notification must be scoped to both notification ID and authenticated recipient user ID.
- Mark-all-read must affect only the authenticated member's notifications.
- A notification must never grant authorization to its target resource.
- Current authorization, membership entitlement, gender/privacy, block/report and visibility rules must be evaluated again when the target route opens.

No browser-supplied notification type, recipient, verification status or moderation decision may be trusted as authoritative communication data.

## 3.3 Moderation transaction boundary

Moderation state is authoritative. In-app notification delivery is downstream from the completed moderation transaction.

For Photo, Aadhaar and Video moderation, the required sequence is:

```text
Validate request and permission
          |
          v
Validate current moderation state
          |
          v
Begin DB transaction
          |
          v
Persist authoritative decision/history/audit as applicable
          |
          v
Commit
          |
          v
Attempt in-app notification
          |
          +--> success: continue
          |
          +--> failure: log operational error; do not undo moderation
          |
          v
Attempt other downstream communication as applicable
```

A notification persistence failure after a successful moderation commit must not cause the application to report the moderation decision as rolled back when it has actually committed. Post-commit notification failures must therefore be handled as best-effort downstream failures and logged appropriately.

Interest workflows may retain their existing established transaction semantics where notification persistence is intentionally part of the authoritative Interest transaction. Do not normalize different domain transactions merely for architectural symmetry.

## 3.4 In-app UI behaviour

The existing bell and notification-list UI is the approved presentation surface. New moderation notification types must reuse it; no parallel notification UI should be created.

Each notification may contain:

- Business-event type/icon treatment supported by the existing UI.
- Short title.
- Concise member-safe message.
- Timestamp.
- Read/unread state.
- Safe internal target route.

Expected moderation targets include the relevant existing Profile/Account Settings area, for example Photo, Aadhaar or Video Introduction management.

The target route remains responsible for its normal access rules and current state. A stale notification may therefore lead to content that has subsequently changed or become unavailable.

## 3.5 Implemented email foundation

The application provides a reusable email foundation including:

- Central `EmailRegistry` definitions with deterministic preview data.
- `MemberEmailService` as the application boundary for normal member email communication.
- `MemberEmailRecipientService` for authoritative member email eligibility.
- `AdminEmailService` for administrative email communication.
- Durable `email_queue` persistence.
- Worker-based asynchronous email delivery.
- Bounded retry handling and stale-job recovery.
- Common branded email layout and reusable components.
- Email Preview Centre using the production renderer.
- Test-send support through the real queue/worker/provider pipeline.
- Shared presentation support for member-facing dates and Indian Rupee formatting.

Implemented standardized email events include:

- Member email verification/replacement verification.
- Administrator invitation.
- Interest received.
- Interest accepted.
- Interest declined.
- Profile photo rejected.
- Aadhaar approved.
- Aadhaar rejected.
- Video Introduction approved.
- Video Introduction rejected.
- Video Introduction resubmission requested.
- Support request received.
- Support request resolved.
- Membership activated/payment confirmation.
- Membership expiring soon reminder.
- Membership expired.

Photo approval intentionally does not currently generate email because it does not require member action and would add unnecessary external communication volume. It does generate an in-app notification.

High-volume Profile Viewed and Shortlisted email communication is handled through the preference/digest architecture rather than immediate email.

## 3.6 Implemented unified communication policy

The application includes a channel-independent communication policy and orchestration layer.

Implemented capabilities include:

- Central `CommunicationEventRegistry`.
- Durable `communication_events`.
- Stable business-event identifiers.
- Central `CommunicationPolicyService`.
- Communication categories.
- Channel-specific delivery decisions.
- Reference-backed event idempotency.
- Member Communication Preferences.
- Matrimonial Activity email preference.
- Engagement frequency preferences: Daily Digest, Weekly Digest and Off.
- `PROFILE_VIEWED` and `PROFILE_SHORTLISTED` event producers.
- Privacy-safe Engagement digest aggregation.
- Frequency-aware recipient batching.
- PostgreSQL row locking with `SKIP LOCKED`.
- Recovery of stale Engagement event reservations.
- Consumption of opted-out Engagement events so disabled communication does not create a future backlog.

The durable email queue remains the Email delivery boundary.

## 3.7 Implemented SMS foundation

SMS is intentionally narrower than Email and in-app communication.

Current implementation includes:

- Central SMS provider abstraction.
- Environment-selectable SMS provider.
- mTalkz provider integration.
- Development/log SMS provider support.
- Standard OTP SMS generation and delivery.
- SMS attempt logging.
- Provider message/reference capture where supplied.
- Safe provider error capture.
- SMS operational visibility.
- OTP request abuse monitoring.

A successful current SMS status means **Accepted by Provider**. It does not mean confirmed handset delivery. DLR/handset-delivery state must not be inferred without a reliable supported callback contract.

## 3.8 Communication Operations and health

Authorized Super Admin users have read-only Communication Operations for Email and SMS.

The combined health state is one of:

- `HEALTHY`
- `WARNING`
- `CRITICAL`

The health view is observational and must not retry communication, mutate queue state, create health records or replace infrastructure/provider monitoring.

# 4. Product principles

## 4.1 One business-event vocabulary

Stable business-event identifiers belong in `CommunicationEventRegistry`.

`MemberNotificationModel` must remain a persistence model and must not define duplicate business-event constants such as Interest, Photo, Aadhaar or Video notification types.

Feature workflows and channel services must use the canonical registry identifiers.

Known events must use their specific identifier rather than being collapsed into `SYSTEM`. `SYSTEM` is reserved for genuinely generic application/system messages that do not have a defined business event.

## 4.2 Business events, not providers, drive communication

Feature workflows should communicate that a business event occurred. They must not contain provider-specific Email/SMS/WhatsApp logic.

The communication architecture determines which channels are appropriate.

## 4.3 Server-side facts are authoritative

Communication content must be generated from authoritative application state. Client-supplied names, membership types, verification statuses, URLs, recipient IDs or relationship states must not be trusted as communication facts.

## 4.4 Privacy before engagement

The objective is not to maximize notifications. The objective is to deliver the minimum useful information through the appropriate channel.

## 4.5 No channel may bypass authorization

A communication may invite a recipient to a feature, but opening it must still execute current authorization rules.

Free/Paid restrictions, Male/Female privacy rules, Interest relationship state, block/report state, profile visibility and resource ownership must never be bypassed because a notification exists.

## 4.6 Optional communication must not roll back committed business state

For normal moderation, support, payment and membership communication, authoritative state is committed first. Optional downstream communication is then attempted.

A channel failure must not normally undo committed business state or cause a misleading failure response for an already committed operation.

Security workflows and existing domain workflows may be explicit exceptions where communication persistence is part of completing the transaction.

# 8. In-app/Application Notification QA requirements

QA must test the notification channel as a backend-authorized feature, not only as a visual bell.

## 8.1 Happy-path checks

For every implemented immediate in-app event:

1. Perform the valid authoritative business action.
2. Confirm the business action succeeds.
3. Confirm exactly the expected notification is created for the correct recipient.
4. Confirm the bell unread count increases appropriately.
5. Confirm the notification appears in recent/full notification UI using the existing presentation.
6. Confirm title/message are member-safe and match the actual event.
7. Confirm the target route is internal and relevant.
8. Open the notification and confirm read state is updated.
9. Confirm the target route independently applies current authorization.

## 8.2 Negative and authorization checks

QA must verify:

- Another authenticated member cannot read or mark a notification they do not own by changing notification ID/direct URL.
- Mark-all-read affects only the current member.
- Invalid/unauthorized moderation attempts create no notification.
- A stale moderation request that no longer matches the pending state creates no duplicate notification.
- Client-side button hiding/disablement is not treated as backend authorization.
- A notification target cannot be used to bypass Free/Paid entitlement.
- Male/Female profile-access restrictions still apply after following a notification.
- Blocked/reported/hidden/unavailable targets are handled by current target-route policy without leaking protected state.
- External/protocol-relative/scheme URLs cannot be persisted through the normal notification service target URL boundary.

## 8.3 Moderation reliability checks

For Photo, Aadhaar and Video moderation:

- Successful moderation commits before downstream notification delivery.
- A simulated notification failure must not revert the moderation state.
- A notification failure must not make the UI claim the moderation decision failed if it has already committed.
- Notification failure must be logged for operational diagnosis.
- Email/downstream channel failure must likewise not restore the old moderation state.

## 8.4 Duplicate checks

QA must verify:

- Repeating an invalid/stale moderation submission does not create another notification.
- Bulk photo approval creates one notification for the bulk action.
- Interest workflow does not create duplicate notifications for invalid repeated state transitions.
- Retry behaviour of external communication does not manufacture duplicate in-app notifications.

## 8.5 Event identity checks

Persisted `notification_type` must use the canonical registry value for known events.

Specifically verify:

- Photo approved = `PHOTO_APPROVED`.
- Photo rejected = `PHOTO_REJECTED`.
- Aadhaar approved = `AADHAAR_APPROVED`.
- Aadhaar rejected = `AADHAAR_REJECTED`.
- Video approved = `VIDEO_APPROVED`.
- Video rejected = `VIDEO_REJECTED`.
- Video resubmission requested = `VIDEO_RESUBMISSION_REQUESTED`.
- Interest received = `INTEREST_RECEIVED`.
- Interest accepted = `INTEREST_ACCEPTED`.
- Interest declined = `INTEREST_DECLINED`.

Known events must not be persisted as `SYSTEM` merely to avoid adding/using the correct registry identifier.

# 9. Member email eligibility and Free vs Paid implications

Normal member email requires a current verified primary EMAIL contact. If none exists, email is skipped without undoing the business event. Email Verification is the intentional exception because verification is the purpose of that email.

Free members may receive legitimate notifications, but communication must not reveal information protected by Paid Full Profile or another entitlement.

Aadhaar Verification is available according to its current entitlement rules and external Aadhaar communication must never expose Aadhaar number, document, stored Aadhaar name or Aadhaar DOB.

Paid status is an entitlement, not consent to more marketing.

The current membership expiry cadence remains one email exactly three member-facing calendar days before expiry and one expiry email after authoritative lifecycle expiry.

---

# 10. Matrimonial privacy requirements

External channels must not unnecessarily include:

- Full phone numbers or email addresses of another member.
- Aadhaar information or document links.
- Private gallery images.
- Video Introduction media URLs.
- Full protected profile data.
- Family contact information.
- Internal moderation notes.
- Raw payment-provider responses.

Existing female-name masking and Male-to-Female access rules continue to apply. Communication must not become an alternate path to protected identity or profile data.

URLs must be application-controlled and authorization must be re-evaluated when opened.

---

# 11. Trust, safety and abuse requirements

Business workflows must prevent invalid repeated state transitions that could be abused to generate notification spam.

Communication generation should be idempotent where appropriate, and high-volume activity should be deduplicated or digested.

Recipient destinations must come from authoritative application state. APIs must not allow arbitrary recipient manipulation or account enumeration.

Dynamic/member-controlled content must be escaped for its destination channel.

No automated communication may reveal who reported/blocked a profile or internal fraud/risk signals.

# 12. Target communication architecture

## 12.1 Conceptual flow

```text
Authoritative Business Workflow
              |
              v
Canonical Business Event
CommunicationEventRegistry
              |
      Communication Policy
              |
    +---------+---------+----------+----------+
    |                   |          |          |
    v                   v          v          v
In-App/Application     Email      SMS      WhatsApp
    |                   |          |          |
    v                   v          v          v
MemberNotification   Email Queue  Provider   Future Queue
Service                 |
    |                   v
    v                 Worker
member_notifications    |
    |                   v
    v                 Provider
Bell / Centre
```

The current application may still have established feature-specific producer wiring while channel orchestration is migrated incrementally. Do not perform a big-bang rewrite merely for architectural uniformity.

## 12.2 In-app boundary

`MemberNotificationService` owns:

- In-app notification validation.
- Canonical notification-type allowlisting.
- Safe internal target normalization.
- Creation of member-visible notification records.
- Recipient-scoped read operations.
- Header/recent notification data through existing model queries.

`MemberNotificationModel` owns persistence/query mechanics only.

The model must not become a second business-event registry.

## 12.3 Email boundary

Normal member email follows:

```text
Committed business state
          |
          v
MemberEmailService
          |
          v
MemberEmailRecipientService
          |
    verified primary EMAIL?
      +---+---+
      |       |
      No      Yes
      |       |
    Skip      v
          EmailRegistry
              |
              v
         EmailQueueService
              |
              v
           Worker
              |
              v
          Mail provider
```

## 12.4 Channel independence

Failure of one optional channel must not normally roll back the underlying business event or imply that another channel failed.

## 12.5 Event identity and idempotency

Where duplicate delivery is harmful, communication should be identifiable by event type, recipient, business entity/reference and occurrence/version where necessary.

The durable email queue and communication-event architecture should retain reference-backed traceability/idempotency.

## 12.6 Transactional outbox direction

The current post-commit best-effort model is acceptable for current channel scope. A stronger transactional outbox may be considered when scale or required multi-channel guarantees justify it. Do not add channel state to matrimonial domain tables merely for architectural completeness.

`CommunicationEventRegistry` is the stable cross-channel business-event vocabulary.

A communication definition should conceptually specify:

- Stable event identifier.
- Category.
- Default title/subject.
- Available/required channels.
- Template identifiers where relevant.
- Priority/retry policy.
- Preference behaviour.
- Preview/sample data where relevant.
- Reference entity type.
- Privacy sensitivity.
- Whether the event may be digested.

Channel persistence models must not duplicate this vocabulary.

# 14. Email design and QA

Member emails use the common branded layout, approved identity/colours, standard CTA/components and shared date/currency presentation helpers.

Email templates must answer:

1. Why did I receive this?
2. What happened?
3. Do I need to do anything?
4. What is the safe next action?

The Email Preview Centre must use the production renderer and deterministic fictional sample data. Authorized test sends must use the real queue/worker/provider pipeline without creating a real matrimonial event.

# 15. SMS requirements

SMS remains deliberately narrow and primarily supports OTP/security workflows.

Feature/security workflows must not contain mTalkz-specific provider logic.

Operational logging must not persist OTP values, full message bodies, API keys or unnecessary raw provider responses.

Current operational status `SENT` represents **Accepted by Provider**, not confirmed handset delivery.

OTP monitoring uses existing authoritative verification records and current configured warning/critical thresholds. Provider outages must not be misclassified as member abuse.

# 16. WhatsApp requirements

WhatsApp remains a future optional channel.

It requires explicit product approval, appropriate consent, provider-approved templates and isolation of provider template identifiers from domain services.

Initial future candidates may include Interest, verification action required, moderation resubmission, membership lifecycle and support updates. High-volume Profile Viewed/Shortlisted notifications should not be enabled by default.

Two-way member WhatsApp communication requires a separate product/safety review.

- Daily Digest.

- Off.

# 18. Important user journeys

## Journey A - Interest received

1. Member A sends a valid Interest to Member B.
2. Interest transaction succeeds under its established transaction rules.
3. Member B receives the authoritative in-app `INTEREST_RECEIVED` notification.
4. Eligible email communication is attempted through the existing email architecture.
5. Following the notification/CTA returns to authenticated application authorization.

## Journey B - Interest accepted/declined

1. Recipient changes Interest state authoritatively.
2. Original sender receives `INTEREST_ACCEPTED` or `INTEREST_DECLINED`.
3. Eligible external communication is attempted.
4. Current relationship/membership/profile authorization still applies when opened.

## Journey C - Photo moderation

1. Authorized admin approves/rejects pending photo state.
2. Decision/audit commits.
3. In-app `PHOTO_APPROVED` or `PHOTO_REJECTED` is attempted downstream.
4. Bulk approval produces one `PHOTO_APPROVED` member notification for the bulk action.
5. Photo rejection email is attempted where currently configured.
6. Notification/email failure must not undo committed moderation.

## Journey D - Aadhaar moderation

1. Authorized admin approves/rejects the current under-review submission.
2. Verification/submission state commits.
3. In-app `AADHAAR_APPROVED` or `AADHAAR_REJECTED` is attempted downstream.
4. Audit and eligible email communication follow established flow.
5. No Aadhaar document/name/DOB is exposed in external communication.
6. Rejected member follows the existing reupload flow; there is no separate current Aadhaar resubmission state.

## Journey E - Video Introduction moderation

1. Authorized admin performs Approved, Rejected or Request Resubmission decision.
2. Video moderation/history state commits.
3. Notification type maps exactly to `VIDEO_APPROVED`, `VIDEO_REJECTED` or `VIDEO_RESUBMISSION_REQUESTED`.
4. In-app notification is attempted downstream.
5. Eligible email is attempted downstream.
6. Video media URL is never exposed externally.

## Journey F - Support and membership

Support and membership communication continues through established authoritative state, central policy and downstream channel boundaries. Runtime membership authorization never depends on a notification, email or cron having executed.

# 19. Edge cases

QA/development must explicitly handle:

1. Notification target changes/becomes unavailable after creation.
2. Recipient account becomes inactive.
3. Recipient attempts to access another member's notification ID.
4. Target profile becomes hidden/blocked/reported after notification creation.
5. Interest state changes before an older notification is opened.
6. Same business action is submitted twice.
7. Moderation notification persistence fails after successful commit.
8. Email/SMS provider fails after successful business commit.
9. Bulk photo approval contains multiple pending photos.
10. Long rejection reasons approach allowed bounds.
11. Member-controlled text contains markup/URLs.
12. Free member follows a notification to a paid-protected destination.
13. Male member follows a notification to a female profile without the required relationship state.
14. Old notification points to a deleted/unavailable entity.
15. Worker/provider retry occurs independently from in-app notification state.
16. Communication preferences change while external communication is queued.

# 20. Admin and operational requirements

Communication Operations remains read-only for the currently approved scope.

Email operations expose queue health and safe event/reference information. SMS operations expose provider acceptance/failure, masked recipient, safe error information and OTP/provider health.

In-app notification persistence failures occurring after committed moderation must be logged with sufficient non-sensitive context to diagnose the member/event/entity without exposing protected Aadhaar/video data.

Manual retry, if introduced later, must be permission-controlled and audited.

---

# 21. Delivery and observability requirements

The communication platform should eventually expose useful metrics for messages/events generated, queue depth, latency, retry/failure rates, provider acceptance, notification volume per member and opt-out rates.

Application notification failures must be distinguishable operationally from failures of the authoritative moderation/business transaction.

Do not use external-channel telemetry to infer matrimonial interest or expose such inference to another member without a separate product decision.

---

# 22. UX/content guidelines

Communication should be respectful, calm, clear, non-judgmental and privacy-conscious.

Prefer one primary action per communication.

Interest decline wording must remain neutral. Moderation rejection/resubmission wording must clearly distinguish status and required action without exposing internal notes.

# 28. Final product recommendation

SikhanandKaraj communication architecture is built around **one canonical business-event vocabulary with separate channel responsibilities**.

In-app/Application Notification should remain the richest authenticated member channel. Its `member_notifications` persistence and read/unread behaviour should remain standalone because this is member-facing application state, while event identity comes from the shared `CommunicationEventRegistry`.

Email remains the durable asynchronous channel for important transactional/activity communication and controlled engagement. SMS remains deliberately narrow and primarily supports OTP/security. WhatsApp remains a future opt-in channel.

Do not merge persistence models merely to reduce the number of classes/tables. Simplicity here means:

- one event vocabulary;
- one in-app notification service/model boundary;
- one established bell/list UI;
- one central policy for channel decisions;
- authoritative business state before optional downstream delivery;
- no provider-specific logic in matrimonial feature services;
- no communication path around privacy, moderation or entitlement rules.

For QA, the most important invariant is that a notification reports an already valid business event; it does not create authorization, does not expose protected information, and a downstream notification failure must not falsify or undo an authoritative moderation decision.
